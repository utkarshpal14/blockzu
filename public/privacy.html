Blockzu — Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: September 2026 | Developer: PriorApp Games

1. Introduction

Welcome to Blockzu ("the Game"), developed and published by PriorApp Games ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles your information when you play on Web, PWA, or Android devices.

2. Data We Collect & Local Storage

Blockzu is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not collect or transmit personal identifying information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number to external game servers.

All gameplay data is stored locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage, including:

  • High scores and total gameplay statistics (games played, lines cleared, blocks placed).
  • Earned in-game currency (Coins) and unlocked cosmetic board themes.
  • Daily login streaks and completed achievement/mission progress.
  • Audio and vibration preferences.
  • Anonymous pre-launch telemetry metrics (average score, revives used, session length).

3. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization

Blockzu may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or rewarded video networks) to support free gameplay.

  • No In-Game Popups: Advertisements never interrupt active block placement rounds.
  • Rewarded Ads: Players may choose to watch rewarded video ads optionally to earn bonus coins or continue a match once per run.
  • Third-Party SDKs: Ad networks may collect anonymous non-personal technical identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, device model, language, IP address, and coarse location) to serve personalized or contextual advertisements in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Blockzu is a family-friendly casual puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

5. Data Security & Retention

Since your progress is stored directly on your device, clearing your browser cache or uninstalling the app will reset your local save data. You have full control over your saved data at all times via your browser or device settings.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
